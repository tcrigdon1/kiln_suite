# Code of Conduct

Kiln Suite is a project for people who like making pictures, videos and software. We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people reporting their first bug.
- Assume good intent, and give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Stay on topic and respect people's time; a short, specific issue beats a long, vague one.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or repeatedly derailing discussions.
- Submitting code or assets you do not have the right to license under this project's MIT License.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project. Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/tcrigdon1). Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break these rules, and will explain the decision to the people involved.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
